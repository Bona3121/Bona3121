# 💫 About Me:
I'm a undergraduate Informatics System student in University Of Indonesia. I’m passionate about Website, Product and Project Management, with strong interests in strategic planning, innovation, and cultural storytelling through technology.<br><br>As the creator of LOHKAN (HKI Holder), a digital platform that promotes the rich culinary heritage of Pangkalpinang, Bangka Belitung, I aspire to bridge tradition and modernity. LOHKAN empowers users to explore authentic local food through features like Explore, Food Review, Bucket List, Ask Recipe, and Gallery, making traditional dishes more accessible, appreciated, and preserved in the digital age.<br><br>My leadership journey is also shaped by my role as Head of Public Relations at HIMASS UI 2024, where I developed strong communication, public engagement, and stakeholder management skills. Through UI Innovation War 2023, I collaborated in driving impactful tech projects focused on creativity and collaboration.<br><br>In 2024, I was honored to be selected as an awardee of the Female Future Leader program by XL Axiata, a leadership development initiative that equips young Indonesian women with the skills, mentorship, and mindset to become future changemakers.<br><br>Outside of tech and management, I’m passionate about public speaking and community empowerment. I thrive in dynamic, purpose-driven environments where I can combine analytical thinking with creative problem-solving to lead meaningful projects that make a lasting impact.
